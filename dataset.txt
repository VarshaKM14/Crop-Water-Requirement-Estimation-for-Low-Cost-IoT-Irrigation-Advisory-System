The dataset consists of 10,000 records with 45 features capturing essential agricultural parameters. It integrates information on weather conditions, soil nutrients, crop management techniques, and external influencing factors. The dataset is valuable for researchers and agronomists seeking to develop predictive models for optimizing crop yields.

Column Description:

Date – Date of data collection
Region – Geographic location of the farmland
Temperature – Average temperature (°C) during the growing period
Humidity – Relative humidity (%) in the region
Rainfall – Total rainfall (mm) during the growing season
SolarRadiation – Amount of sunlight received (W/m²)
SoilMoisture – Moisture level in soil (%)
SoilpH – Soil pH level (acidity/alkalinity)
SoilOrganicMatter – Organic content in soil (%)
NitrogenContent – Nitrogen (N) level in soil (mg/kg)
PhosphorusContent – Phosphorus (P) level in soil (mg/kg)
PotassiumContent – Potassium (K) level in soil (mg/kg)
CalciumContent – Calcium (Ca) level in soil (mg/kg)
MagnesiumContent – Magnesium (Mg) level in soil (mg/kg)
SulfurContent – Sulfur (S) level in soil (mg/kg)
IronContent – Iron (Fe) level in soil (mg/kg)
ZincContent – Zinc (Zn) level in soil (mg/kg)
CopperContent – Copper (Cu) level in soil (mg/kg)
BoronContent – Boron (B) level in soil (mg/kg)
ManganeseContent – Manganese (Mn) level in soil (mg/kg)
EC – Electrical conductivity of soil (dS/m)
CationExchangeCapacity – Soil’s ability to retain nutrients (meq/100g)
BulkDensity – Density of soil (g/cm³)
CropType – Type of crop cultivated
SowingDate – Date of crop sowing
HarvestDate – Date of harvesting
GrowthDuration – Number of days between sowing and harvesting
SeedVariety – Type of seed used for cultivation
FertilizerType – Type of fertilizer applied
FertilizerAmount – Quantity of fertilizer used (kg/ha)
PesticideType – Type of pesticide applied
PesticideAmount – Quantity of pesticide used (kg/ha)
IrrigationMethod – Type of irrigation used (e.g., drip, flood)
IrrigationFrequency – Number of irrigation cycles per season
TillageMethod – Type of tillage method used (e.g., plowing, zero tillage)
WeedControlMethod – Weed management technique used
HarvestingMethod – Method used for harvesting (manual/machine)
LaborCount – Number of workers involved in farming
Yield – Crop yield obtained (kg/ha)
MarketPrice – Selling price of the harvested crop (currency/unit)
ProductionCost – Total cost of production (currency/ha)
ProfitMargin – Net profit per hectare (currency)
StorageDuration – Number of days the yield was stored before selling
TransportationCost – Cost of transporting produce to the market (currency)
PestInfectionLevel – Severity of pest infestation (low, medium, high)
